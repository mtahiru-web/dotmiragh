## 1. Set up Google Search Console for dotmiragh.com

Only 7 of the last 49 visitors came from Google, and the SEO review shows Search Console is not connected, blocking indexing monitoring and sitemap submission.

**Next step:** Authorize the Google Search Console connector, verify the site with the META tag, and submit `https://dotmiragh.com/sitemap.xml`.

Semrush (Ghana database) shows 210 monthly searches for "web hosting Ghana" with easy keyword difficulty (16/100) and a $1.05 CPC, matching your existing hosting service.

**Next step:** Expand the existing Web Hosting section or create a dedicated landing page targeting "web hosting Ghana" with clear pricing, features, and a contact CTA.

Your `/excel-tracker` page already gets traffic, and Semrush (US database) shows low-competition question keywords such as "how to track business expenses and income in excel" (30/mo, $6.83 CPC) and "how to track inventory for small business in excel" (20/mo, $6.87 CPC).

**Next step:** Turn the existing `/excel-tracker` page into a practical guide that answers these questions and offers your custom tracker as the done-for-you solution.
